Throughout my internship at **Amazon Web Services Vietnam LLC** from **May 04, 2026** to **July 30, 2026**, I had the opportunity to experience a professional working environment, study and practice AWS cloud computing knowledge, and gradually apply these concepts to a real-world project. This internship helped me bridge the gap between classroom theory and enterprise-level deployment.

Throughout the internship, I went through distinct learning and working phases. In the initial phase, I focused on getting familiar with the workspace, setting up my AWS account, preparing the necessary tools, and studying foundational topics such as Cloud Computing, AWS Global Infrastructure, IAM, Billing, and Cost Management. This was an essential foundation that helped me understand how AWS organizes resources, operates systems, and supports businesses in deploying cloud infrastructure.

In the following weeks, I progressed to researching and practicing core AWS service groups including Networking, Compute, Storage, Security, Databases, and Data Analytics. By combining theoretical study with hands-on labs, I gained a deeper understanding of building VPC networks, deploying EC2 servers, utilizing S3 for data storage, managing permissions with IAM, encrypting data with KMS, implementing database systems with RDS/Aurora, and getting introduced to data analytics services like Glue, Athena, DynamoDB, and QuickSight. I was also highly mindful of cost tracking and made sure to clean up resources after practical sessions to prevent unexpected expenses.

In the final phase of the internship, I shifted my focus from learning AWS services to applying them to the AI Image Upscaling Service project. I explored the Frontend codebase built on Next.js 14 App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, and react-dropzone; analyzed the project structure; finalized the API Contract between Frontend and Backend; built the image upload UI; configured the AI/Standard processing modes; set up the API Client; integrated the image processing endpoints; and handled loading, success, and error states to display the processed results. This hands-on experience gave me practical insights into the complete feature development workflow of a real-world project.

Through this internship, I improved several key skills, including reading technical documentation, conducting hands-on labs, taking notes, reporting progress, reading codebases, analyzing requirements, integrating APIs, functional testing, and troubleshooting errors. Furthermore, I developed a deeper appreciation for organized planning, time management, process compliance, and compiling technical documentation after deployment.

Regarding my work ethic, I always strove to complete my assigned tasks, took guidance from my supervisor seriously, and proactively researched new topics independently. However, I recognize that I still need to improve my communication skills, present issues more concisely, and collaborate more proactively with the team during work.
