### General Review

**1. Internship Environment**
 During my internship at Amazon Web Services Vietnam Company Limited, I had the opportunity to experience a professional and well-structured working environment. The learning curriculum was organized in clear phases, ranging from foundational cloud computing knowledge to hands-on labs and real-world application projects. This helped me easily track my progress, take more initiative in my learning, and understand how cloud technology is applied in practice.
**2. Guidance and Support**
 Throughout the internship, I received the necessary support from my mentor and colleagues in the department. Whenever I encountered difficulties while learning AWS or researching the project, I was guided on how to analyze the issue, look up documentation, and self-verify the results. As a result, I gradually improved my self-learning capability, my way of asking questions, and my ability to troubleshoot errors that arose during hands-on practice.

**3. Alignment with Major/Field of Study**  
The internship content is highly relevant to my major in Information Systems. The knowledge I acquired at university, such as computer networking, databases, system security, and application development, was directly connected to AWS services. Learning and practicing on the cloud platform gave me a clearer understanding of how components like network infrastructure, servers, storage, permissions, databases, and data analytics collaborate in a complete system.

**4. Learning Opportunities and Skill Development**  
The internship helped me expand my knowledge across various AWS service categories, including Networking, Compute, Storage, Security, Databases, and Data Analytics. Additionally, I had the opportunity to apply this knowledge to the **AI Image Upscaling Service** project through tasks such as exploring the source code, analyzing APIs, building the image upload interface, integrating Frontend with Backend, and testing the end-to-end workflow. Through this, I further developed skills in reading technical documentation, task planning, debugging, and report writing.

**5. Work Ethic and Professionalism**  
The internship process heightened my awareness of the importance of responsibility and proactivity. Every week came with specific goals, deliverables, and self-evaluation sections. This helped me build a habit of taking notes, double-checking completed work, and planning next steps.

**6. Internship Support Conditions**  
I highly appreciate that the internship program offers a clear learning roadmap, abundant reference materials, and accessible hands-on labs. The combination of theoretical learning, practice, and project application helped me not only grasp the concepts but also know how to apply them to concrete situations.

### Additional Questions

**What were you most satisfied with during the internship?**

What satisfied me the most was being able to systemize my knowledge of AWS and progressively apply it to a real-world project. Prior to the internship, my understanding of cloud computing was purely conceptual. After this internship, I gained a much clearer grasp of the roles that services like VPC, EC2, S3, IAM, RDS, DynamoDB, Glue, Athena, and QuickSight play in building a cloud-based system.
Additionally, participating in the **AI Image Upscaling Service** project gave me valuable hands-on experience regarding the Frontend-Backend workflow, especially in API alignment, sending requests, handling response statuses, and displaying results to users.

**2. What can the company improve for future interns?**  
In my opinion, the program could introduce a brief onboarding session for new interns. The content could cover how to read documentation, take notes on hands-on labs, report progress, follow the protocol for asking questions when encountering errors, and note key practices when using AWS resources to avoid unexpected costs. This would help interns get up to speed much quicker in their first few weeks.

**3. If recommending this to friends, would you advise them to intern here?**
Yes, definitely. I believe this is a highly suitable environment for those who want to learn about cloud computing, system infrastructure, and application development. The internship program provides students with a structured learning roadmap, hands-on experience with AWS services, and a deeper understanding of how their academic major is applied in a real-world professional setting.

### Proposals and requests

**Recommendations for improving the internship experience**

I hope that in future cohorts, the company could organize a final review or sharing session where interns can briefly present what they have learned, the challenges they faced, and their achieved outcomes. This activity would help students hone their presentation skills while gaining valuable feedback from mentors and experienced professionals.

**Do you wish to continue developing in this direction?**

Following this internship, I find myself significantly more interested in Cloud Computing and cloud-native application development. I aspire to delve deeper into AWS, focusing specifically on system deployment, security, databases, serverless architectures, CI/CD, and monitoring.

**Other Feedback**

This internship has equipped me with invaluable knowledge and practical experience. I would like to express my sincere gratitude to **Amazon Web Services Vietnam Company Limited**, my mentor, and all the team members who supported me throughout this journey. These experiences form a crucial foundation for me to continue refining my professional skills and shaping my career path in the future.
